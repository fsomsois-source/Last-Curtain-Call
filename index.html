LONDON, 1894
The Last Curtain Call

17 February 1894. Minutes before the grand premiere of The Lady of the Mask at Theatre Royal Drury Lane, a celebrated actress is found dead in her dressing room.

Scotland Yard closes the case within days. Suicide, they say.

But Eleanor Marsh did not die by her own hand. Someone in Covent Garden knew the truth about who really wrote the play. Someone stood to gain everything from her silence.

138 years later, it is your turn to uncover the truth.

⏱ HOW IT WORKS
1You have 105 minutes to solve the case.
2Your team will visit 4 locations across London. Use your phones to find your way, or ask passers-by.
3Detective Pfister and Detective Somsois will give you the code that unlocks your first destination and your first challenges.
4Once on site, solve the riddle and complete the challenge.
5Roles rotate and you start again.
6Each team unlocks different pieces of evidence. At the final challenge, all teams pool their clues together: only as a group can you solve the case. / Chaque équipe débloque des indices différents, mis en commun à la fin : c'est un travail de groupe.
7The final challenge takes place at Covent Garden.

⚠ CRITICAL · YOUR GAME PHONE
  • ALWAYS keep the same phone and the same browser from the very start to the very end of the game.
  • Your progress is saved automatically on this phone. If the phone dies (battery, etc.), do not panic: turn it back on, reopen the game link, everything will come back.
  • NEVER clear your history, cache or browser data: you would lose all your progress.
  • Do not use private browsing.
  • Warning: the countdown timer keeps running even if the phone is off. Real time does not stop.
⚠ GOOD TO KNOW
  • Be very careful when crossing the street.
  • Watch your bags and secure your phones when holding them.
  • Wrist strap is mandatory.
  • Two phones to use: one for the game, the other for photo and video challenges. The 3rd stays in your bag in case of trouble.
  • Riddle answers are always in English, in a single word.

YOUR MISSION

You hold the letters . You must capture them in photos:

  • One letter formed with your bodies (one or more team members).
  • The other letter formed by a passer-by, using their hands, with one or more of your team members visible next to them in the photo.

One letter per phone: the two photos must be taken on two different phones. Since the game phone cannot take photos, another phone is allowed for this challenge.

Then join the other teams at the meeting point. Once all six teams have gathered, your photos will be checked.

All six teams together hold 12 letters. Put end to end, they spell a single word: that is the final word, it is up to you to find it.

When your photos are approved (and only then), type that word in the answer box.
