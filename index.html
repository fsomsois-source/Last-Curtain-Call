LONDON, 1894
The Last Curtain Call

17 February 1894. Minutes before the grand premiere of The Lady of the Mask at Theatre Royal Drury Lane, a celebrated actress is found dead in her dressing room.

Scotland Yard closes the case within days. Suicide, they say.

But Eleanor Marsh did not die by her own hand. Someone in Covent Garden knew the truth about who really wrote the play. Someone stood to gain everything from her silence.

138 years later, it is your turn to uncover the truth.

The Rules

You have 105 minutes to solve the case. Each team follows a unique route of 4 stops through Covent Garden.

At each stop, Lou et Luca will give you a starting code to unlock your first balise. Subsequent codes are generated by the coordinator at the hub.

Complete your photo/video mission, solve the riddle, and collect your evidence. You have 20 minutes per stop — unused time goes to your time bank.

The Joker 🇫🇷 translates a riddle into French. One use per game.

After all 4 stops, meet at the Punch & Judy Pub, East Piazza for the final enigma.
